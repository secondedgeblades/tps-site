Second Edge Blades

A dystopian fiction universe delivered as multimedia packets. Each release combines prose, original music, illustrations, and interactive narrative into a single collectible experience.

The story follows a band that weaponises music and analog technology against a global system of neuroacoustic control. All combat is through sound: SPL, resonance, harmonic distortion, phase alignment. The flaw is the feature.
