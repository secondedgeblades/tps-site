Ruby and the Garden Guardian

A children's picture book about courage, curiosity, and the magic hiding in your own back garden.

It started as one of those small family inventions that could easily have disappeared by the next morning. A walk, a made-up world, a few characters with names, and the feeling that this one deserved to stay. I turned that evening into a real book rather than letting it vanish like most good ideas do.
