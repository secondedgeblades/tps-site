Hop Twice

Instrumental music built from CK's back catalogue of original compositions. Each track is reimagined through AI-assisted production into long-form mixes designed for focus, study, and deep work.

The source material spans 20 years of songwriting across rock, electronic, acoustic, and everything between. The output is something new: familiar enough to disappear into the background, textured enough to reward attention.

How It Works

Original tracks are fed through Suno's AI models with carefully tuned style prompts and parameters. The AI reimagines the harmonic and rhythmic structure while preserving the character of the original. A hard rock anthem becomes a swinging trip hop piece. A solo acoustic guitar ballad picks up dusty breaks and Rhodes keys.

Each mix runs roughly an hour, paired with looping pencil-sketch animations featuring a floppy rabbit who occasionally hops.

Styles

Trip Hop

Dusty vinyl, jazz samples, slow breakbeats, thick atmosphere. The first collection: 15 tracks, 59 minutes.
