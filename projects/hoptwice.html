Hop Twice

The Artist draws. The rabbit sits, mostly. The music plays.

Hop Twice turns twenty years of original songs into long-form instrumental worlds built for focus, study, and late-night thinking.

The source material comes from across the catalogue, then gets reworked into something softer, slower, and more immersive. The point is not nostalgia. The point is atmosphere you can live inside for an hour at a time.

How It Works

Each release begins with one of my own compositions, then pushes it through a different musical lens until it becomes a new listening space with the old bones still inside it. A hard rock song can come back as trip hop. An acoustic piece can return covered in dust, tape hiss, and electric piano.

The mixes run long on purpose. They are built to stay with you rather than ask for your attention every thirty seconds. The rabbit helps.
