Roadmap

What the satchel is actively building, shaping, and carrying forward.

This is not a production board. It is a public snapshot of where the energy is going right now.

Some work moves fast. Some work takes longer because it needs to. The point of this page is not to promise dates. It is to show what is alive.

Now

Second Edge Blades

The next packet is the main line of fire. Prose leads. The rest assembles around it.

Music, visuals, and the interactive edges follow once the core piece is locked.

Taking Shape

Soft Dark and Hop Twice

The Soft Dark is growing into a fuller world of creatures, art objects, and small physical things. Hop Twice continues to turn the back catalogue into long-form listening pieces.

These projects are active, but they move in support of the larger studio rhythm rather than dictating it.

On Deck

Merch, Objects, and Experiments

Physical goods, odd artifacts, and format experiments are part of the plan, but only when they earn their place.

The studio is interested in new forms. It is not interested in filling shelves for the sake of looking busy.
