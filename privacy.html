Privacy Policy

This site collects only the information you choose to send through the newsletter signup form or the custom project inquiry form.

Form submissions are processed by FormSubmit and forwarded to the studio inbox at secondedgeblades@gmail.com. That means your message contents, email address, and any other details you enter pass through FormSubmit in order to reach the inbox.

Newsletter signups submitted through this site are handled manually. Submitting the form does not create a direct Substack account on your behalf. It sends your email address to the studio inbox so the request can be handled there.

This site also links to third-party services including Substack, Etsy, Blurb, Printify-hosted product pages, Tumblr, and YouTube. If you follow those links, their privacy practices apply on their own sites.

No effort is made here to sell, rent, or trade your information. If you want a form submission deleted, send a request to secondedgeblades@gmail.com.
