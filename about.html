About

CK Armstrong is a musician, writer, and visual artist who got tired of pretending those were separate jobs. The Piglet's Satchel is the place where the work gets to stay whole. Stories can carry music with them. Songs can point back to worlds. Drawings can stop being decoration and become part of the thing itself.

Most of the work here starts from the same instinct: make something honest, make it with care, and leave enough of the fingerprints on it that another human being can feel there was a person on the other side. Some projects are dark. Some are soft. Some are strange enough to need their own pocket.

This site is the front door to that collection. Have a rummage.
