About

CK is a musician, writer, and maker of things. Twenty years of professional music experience taught him that the best work happens when you stop separating disciplines and let them bleed into each other. Fiction carries rhythm. Music tells stories. Art makes both land harder.

The Piglet's Satchel is where all of it lives. It's a studio, not a brand. The work comes first; the satchel just holds it together and gives you somewhere to rummage.
