About

I like to make stuff and have a very scattered brain. So if something gets created that makes me feel some kinda way, it goes in 'the satchel' and anyone who cares can have a rummage.

This is a dream, a hobby, a side project. I'm working hard on my craft and if you stick around long enough, you can watch me improve.

Drop me an email, show me what you're working on, tell me I suck, tell me your favorite piece. I'd love to hear it all. In the meantime, have a rummage.
